# Privacy Policy for BalaKatha

**Last Updated: August 21, 2026**

BalaKatha ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting the privacy of children and parents who use our mobile application ("BalaKatha" or "the App"). This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, stored, and protected when you interact with our App.

---

## 1. Overview & Children's Privacy (COPPA & GDPR-K Compliance)

BalaKatha is designed for children and families. We strictly comply with the **Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA)**, the **General Data Protection Regulation for Children (GDPR-K)**, and Google Play’s **Families Policy Requirements**.

- **No Behavioral Advertising**: BalaKatha does not contain any third-party behavioral or targeted advertisements.
- **No Selling of Personal Data**: We never sell, rent, or trade any user information to third parties.

---

## 2. Information Collected and How It Is Used

### A. Local Profile Personalization (Stored Exclusively On-Device)
To personalize story experiences (e.g. inserting family names into bedtime and moral tales), parents or children can enter:
- Kid's Name
- Father's Name
- Mother's Name
- Preferred Narration Language
- Reader Gender / Preference

> **Note**: This information is stored **locally on your device** using secure local storage (`AsyncStorage` / `EncryptedStorage`). It is never transmitted to, stored on, or harvested by external servers.

### B. Anonymous Diagnostics & Analytics
To ensure app stability and performance, we utilize minimal third-party diagnostic services:
- **Firebase Crashlytics**: Collects anonymized technical stack traces when app crashes occur to help us fix technical errors.
- **PostHog Analytics**: Collects aggregated, non-personally identifiable usage statistics (e.g. screens viewed) to improve user experience.

*We do not collect Advertising IDs (AAID/IDFA) or precise location data from child users.*

---

## 3. Data Retention and Deletion

Because profile information is stored on your device, you have complete control over your data:

- **Resetting Profile**: You can clear all saved profile names, quiz scores, and unlocked badges at any time by navigating to **Settings > System Controls > Reset Application**.
- **App Uninstallation**: Uninstalling BalaKatha permanently removes all locally stored application data from your device.

---

## 4. Third-Party Service Providers

Our App uses trusted service providers solely for story distribution and app performance:

- **Supabase**: Used as a secure cloud database to retrieve read-only story text and artwork assets.
- **Google Firebase**: Used for crash reporting and technical performance diagnostics.

---

## 5. Security

We take reasonable precautions to protect the data stored on your device. We restrict access to local device storage using standard operating system encryption protocols.

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" revision date.
